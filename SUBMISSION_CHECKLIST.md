# Final submission checklist

## Prototype brief (short)

**Problem:** KSP officers need quick FIR/SCRB answers (pending by PS, hotspots, repeat accused) and next actions on Catalyst.  

**Features:** NLP search, briefing, maps + Open in Maps, accused history, case desk, patrol, contacts.  

**Stack:** Zoho Catalyst (function + web client + Data Store), Node.js, HTML/JS, Chart.js, GeoJSON, Maps links.  

**Impact:** Faster shortlist and field follow-up for IO/SHO without leaving the app.
